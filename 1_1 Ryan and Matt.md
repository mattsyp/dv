## Purpose of This Document

This document exists to support consistent, high-quality 1:1 conversations over time. It is a shared working space designed to keep discussions focused on growth, clarity and alignment rather than status updates. The engineer owns the agenda and brings the topics that matter most each week. The manager uses this document to capture observations, feedback themes and follow-ups so important context is not lost. Notes and action items are recorded to create continuity between meetings and to prevent surprises later in reviews or performance conversations. This document is not meant to solve problems in a single meeting. Its value comes from being used consistently as a record of progress, decisions and shared understanding.
