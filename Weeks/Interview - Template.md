# [Datavant's 5 Core Values](https://loop.datavant.com/workspaces/general/our-values)

## 1\. We are customer obsessed. 

We listen to our customers’ current needs, anticipate their future needs, and set the pace for the possible. Today's awesome is tomorrow's average.

* Tell me about a time when you anticipated a customer’s future needs before they expressed them. How did you identify this need, and what steps did you take to address it?  
* Describe a situation where you had to elevate the standard of service for a customer. What was your approach?  
* How do you balance pushing back on a customer when needed while maintaining a strong partnership?   
* How do you find win-win solutions?

## 2\. We prioritize growth over comfort. 

Growth happens outside our comfort zone. We embrace ambition, strive for exceptionalism, and see feedback as a gift. We invest boldly to grow each other, our network, and our impact on healthcare.

* Tell me about a role or a goal you took on that felt “too big” and how you addressed it.  
* Tell me about a time when you prioritized growth over comfort at work.  
  * What would you do differently if you could do it all over again?  
* Teach me about a technical concept you’re passionate about.   
  * How & why did you learn this?

## 3\. We earn trust. 

Every datapoint we touch represents a patient. We obsess over patient privacy and benefit in all decisions we make, even when it adds friction.

* In your previous work, how have you ensured that the human aspect behind data wasn’t lost? Can you provide an example of a time when keeping “data human” led to a better decision or outcome?  
* Describe your approach to explaining complex data findings to non-technical stakeholders or patients.   
  * How do you ensure that the human impact of the data is clearly communicated?  
* Tell me about a time when you simplified a complex process or workflow? What was your approach and what was the impact on team or customer efficiency?

## 4\. We win as a team.  

We demonstrate unmatched accountability and follow-through. We lead with “how might we?” rather than “we can’t.” We raise a hand when there is no clear owner and embrace a “no job too big, no job too small” mindset to win together.

* What’s something outside your job description that you’ve done to accelerate your team?  
  * What results did it yield?  
  * How did the team react?  
* What’s a project you drove from 0 to 1 that delivered results in a past job?  
  * What was a metric that spurred you to take action?  
  * What would you have done differently if you had only ½ the resources?  
* Tell me about a time you went out of your way to help someone feel included.   
  * Why is this important to the team’s culture and success?

## 5\. We rise to the challenge. 

We respect team and customer time by simplifying steps, knowing when done is better than perfect, and never being the bottleneck. We speak truthfully and openly so information travels fast.

* Tell me about a time you noticed an inefficiency and took steps to improve productivity.   
* Describe a situation where you had to balance quality with speed. How did you navigate competing priorities?

# Questions to Ask

## \[Not Asked\] Tell me about one of the most technically difficult projects you've worked on where you personally had significant responsibility. Walk me through the problem, the architecture, and the hardest technical decisions you had to make.

* What specifically made it difficult?  
* What alternatives did you consider?  
* What did you decide versus the team?  
* What tradeoffs did you knowingly make?  
* Where did the design turn out to be wrong?  
* If you were designing it today, what would you change?

**Primary Value:** We rise to the challenge  
**Signal:** Core
